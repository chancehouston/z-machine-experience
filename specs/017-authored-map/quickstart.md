# Quickstart: An Authored Map for Zork I

```sh
cargo test --workspace
scripts/build-ios.sh
scripts/test-ios.sh 27
scripts/test-ios.sh 26
```

By hand, on the owner's phone (SC-006), from a new game: wander the forest, walk
round the house, go inside, up to the attic and down to the cellar and the first
rooms beyond. Does the page read as a map of the place? Do the gaps trouble you? Is
anything where you would not have put it?
