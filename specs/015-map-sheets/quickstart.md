# Quickstart: The Other Sheets of the Map

```sh
cargo test --workspace
scripts/build-ios.sh
scripts/test-ios.sh 27
scripts/test-ios.sh 26
```

Run the two app suites in turn, with nothing else building.

By hand, on the owner's phone (SC-005): go down to the Cellar and on a few rooms,
open the map, turn back to the sheet with the house on it by the way that leads up
or by an arrow, find a room visited earlier, and return to where you are.
